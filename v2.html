About me

When everyone can make things, taste is the differentiator.

AI raises the floor for everyone. But not the ceiling. In this era, taste and judgement are the real currency — knowing what's worth building, and caring enough to build it right. Making something is no longer the hard part. Knowing whether it's any good still is, and that's where most of my time goes.

How I work

01

Design at scale

I lead teams, build design systems, and work across whole product ecosystems. At Formalize I grew the design function from 0 to 4 and built the design system from scratch across 2 products. At Maersk I was first design lead on one platform serving three user types and 20+ product teams.

02

Player-coach

I hire for talent density, mentor closely, and stay in the craft myself rather than reviewing it from a distance. I still believe the most powerful thing a design leader can do is help someone grow into a version of themselves they didn't know was possible.

03

Maker

The best design decisions I've made weren't about how something looked. They were about what to build at all. I stay hands-on and use AI heavily to get to something clickable early. I'd rather hand a team a working prototype than a flat frame, because that's the only way to tell how an interaction actually feels.

Experience

I work hands-on across product design and product strategy — discovery and framing, concepts, high-fidelity design, and design systems. After 8 years leading design teams, most recently building the function from 0 at Formalize and leading a team of 12 at Maersk, I now take that work on directly rather than through a team. I am used to joining an existing setup and working the way it already works: the stack, the rituals and the constraints as they are, with very little onboarding. Engagements run from a few weeks on a specific problem to ongoing fractional design leadership.

Joined as Formalize's first design leader, inheriting 2 products built fast and engineering-first — no PMs, no triads, and a previous design-system attempt that hadn't stuck. Grew the team from 0 to 4, embedded designers directly in product triads, and rebuilt the design system with engineering this time, so what got designed is what actually shipped. Helped craft the pitch behind a €30M Series B and led the internal deck that aligned the company on 2025 product direction. 2 years in, design moved from an occasional freelance favour to fully embedded — and Whistleblower Software became the #1-rated platform on G2.

Led UX across a platform serving 350+ people across 20+ product teams and 260 engineers. Grew the design function from 6 to 12 in just 6 months and introduced a new organisational structure across 3 sub-teams: Product Design, Design System, and Research. Championed earlier involvement of UX in problem-solving, elevated design standards across the organisation, and initiated the "Flea Market" design system to modernise Maersk's existing framework. Successfully advocated for UX moving from under Technology into Product — a structural shift that changed how design influenced decisions platform-wide. Maersk was named one of Fast Company's Most Innovative Companies in Design 2023.

Brought in as Head of UX as Hilding Anders Group transformed its Sleepacy brand from a global mattress manufacturer into a digital-first health and wellness company. Managed digital experience across both SLEEPACY (Sleep Health) and Carpe Diem Beds (luxury). Conceptualised and designed an e-commerce site from scratch, led the "Sleep Services" app from concept through to validated prototypes, and directed external design and development agencies to align on the vision for digital offerings.

Led UX and strategy across 1508's most prominent accounts. Led UX for Club Matas' new customer-facing app and loyalty programme — now Denmark's largest loyalty club with 1.5M members and a 4.6 App Store rating (32,800+ reviews). Drove Finansforbundet's full digital transformation — conceptualising, designing, and optimising 5+ new digital products and thinking them into a coherent ecosystem for 50,000 members. Designed the Dansk Metal job-finding app — used weekly by 66% of all unemployed members, with an iOS rating of 4.0.

7 months in New York at Firstborn — named #7 best US design studio, known for purpose-driven craft with forward-looking global brands. Worked between the UX and strategy teams to get them to a shared idea of what strategic design meant in practice. Led a new business venture: a skincare platform that gave each customer a skin ID to narrow down what to buy. Conducted comprehensive UX research and conversion optimisation for Ted Baker's e-commerce platform, and delivered internal training on design thinking, agile methodologies, and hypothesis-driven design.

4 years translating deep human insights into new business concepts and digital products. Led UX and strategy for Danske Bank's "Pocket Money" app — a pioneering debit card and money management solution for children aged 8–14, the first of its kind from a major Danish bank. Directed UX for the Varier active lifestyle app, which won the Experimental award at the Danish Digital Awards 2016 and was showcased at IFA Berlin — the world's largest consumer tech trade show. Instrumental in growing the agency (then Hello Group) from 20–30 people to 65+ UX experts, culminating in the merger with Great Works and joining The North Alliance consortium of the top Nordic digital agencies.

Worked closely with the CEO and Managing Partner to drive business development and lead major new business pitches. Advised clients on customer experience strategy and mobile app technology. Designed the UX for a platform facilitating the BMW i3 launch in partnership with YouTube. Developed customer support concepts and a child safety app concept for Danish telco TDC. Hello Great Works later invested a 25% stake in Nodes — which led directly to being headhunted into what became NoA Ignite.

Led digital campaign strategy and creative production for major FMCG brands, bridging offline events with digital and social execution. Designed the presentation for the Somersby Cider launch at the National Carlsberg Sales Meeting — the largest gathering of all Carlsberg brands and sales representatives. Campaign work across Coca-Cola, Carlsberg, Tuborg, Mars and Snickers.

Co-founded an online-only prescription eyewear brand with a buy-one-donate-one model, taking on Denmark's conventional optical market. Led brand, marketing and logistics through launch from Shanghai, established a local optician partnership, and sourced the first collection on the ground in China.

Interned at a brand consultancy, on client work for American Express and Skype. Built creative tools, ran co-creation workshops, and worked on concept development and research.

A 3-year hybrid business, design and leadership programme, ranked among the world's most innovative schools by BusinessWeek and FastCompany. Focused on leadership, entrepreneurship and project-based learning in real-world contexts — which is where both the Wolff Olins internship and Riot Bernard came from, each running inside these 3 years.
