How I work

01

Design at scale

I lead teams, build design systems, and work across whole product ecosystems. At Formalize I grew the design function from 0 to 4 and built the design system from scratch across 2 products. At Maersk I was first design lead on one platform serving three user types and 20+ product teams.

02

Player-coach

I hire for talent density, mentor closely, and stay in the craft myself rather than reviewing it from a distance. I still believe the most powerful thing a design leader can do is help someone grow into a version of themselves they didn't know was possible.

03

Maker

The best design decisions I've made weren't about how something looked. They were about what to build at all. I stay hands-on and use AI heavily to get to something clickable early. I'd rather hand a team a working prototype than a flat frame, because that's the only way to tell how an interaction actually feels.

Beyond the work

High trust, low ego

Work takes up too much of your life to spend it with people who make it miserable. The culture I care about building is one where we bring a bit of ourselves to work — where we tell stories, form real relationships, and show up for each other. Vulnerability creates safety, and safety creates the conditions for the best work people have in them.

Guardian of positivism

Someone once called me a guardian of positivism. Energy is contagious, and a team with the right attitude consistently outperforms one without it. I've finished three Ironmans and race competitively in Master A; the attitude that gets you across that finish line is the one that keeps a team moving when things get hard.

Craft and care

I'm fascinated by things that are excessively well made, digital and physical alike — when you can feel that someone cared about making an experience as good as it could be. That's what I want to build, because users choose you over the competition when you solve something real in their lives.
