Case study

Matas

Denmark's largest loyalty club, rebuilt around what members actually said they wanted.

Role

Lead UX Designer

Industry

Retail, beauty

Scope

Loyalty programme, app, research

Timeline

1508, for Matas

What research revealed

Members told us the problems went deeper than the app.

Nine findings came out of research across member segments: half received little value from the reward programme, several of the club's elements no longer worked, and the whole thing needed simplifying.

The challenge

One programme had to work for two very different kinds of member.

Major inequalities sat at the top and bottom of the hierarchy — the bottom half of members and the top 1% spent about the same. Different frequencies needed different value.

The turn

So what should the new club be like?

Members had told us what was broken. The same research told us what they wanted instead, and those three answers set the brief for the whole redesign.

The playbook

01

Two mechanics, one club

Two parallel loyalty mechanics: simple points for lower tiers, choice-based rewards for high-frequency members — one programme that did not ask everyone to behave the same way.

02

Rewards that suit the member

Earned on every purchase. 1 point = 1 kr, for everyone — then choice layered on top for the members who shop often.

03

150 screens, one system

Every member flow designed end to end, so the programme and the app told the same story.
